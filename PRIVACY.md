# Privacy Policy for Jöppli

**Last updated:** August 20, 2026

Welcome to **Jöppli** ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide doorstep recycling collection services and recycling guidance in the Canton of Glarus, Switzerland. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your personal data when you use the Jöppli Android application and related services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use Jöppli, we collect the following categories of information to fulfill our service:

1. **Account & Contact Information**:
   - Name, email address, and phone number when you register or sign in using Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In, Email, or Phone verification).
2. **Address & Location Information**:
   - Your home or pickup address and geographic coordinates to schedule, coordinate, and route doorstep recycling collections.
3. **Photos (AI Recycling Scanner)**:
   - Photos taken with your device camera when using the AI Vision Scanner to identify materials and determine Canton Glarus recycling rules. Images are processed temporarily for classification and are not used for advertising or marketing.
4. **App Activity & Preferences**:
   - Language preferences, selected app theme, recycling statistics (recycled kg and estimated CO2 saved), and pickup booking history stored securely in Firebase Firestore and local device storage.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for the following purposes:
- Coordinating and routing doorstep collection pickups to your designated address.
- Authenticating your user session securely via Firebase Authentication.
- Displaying your recycling statistics and order history in the personal dashboard.
- Providing recycling guidance and instant AI item classification.
- Customer support and communication regarding scheduled pickups.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We utilize trusted infrastructure partners strictly for app functionality:
- **Google Firebase (Google LLC)**: Authentication, Firestore database storage, and AI processing (Firebase AI Logic).
- **Google Maps Platform (Google LLC)**: Places address autocomplete and geographic routing.

All data transmitted between the Jöppli application and our servers is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information as long as your account is active. You may request account and data deletion at any time by contacting us at:
- **Email**: [kontakt@joeppli.ch](mailto:kontakt@joeppli.ch)

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your account records and associated personal data from our systems.
